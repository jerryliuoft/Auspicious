05 Your calendar

Connect your work calendar and each page lines up your meetings with the day: which land in good or careful hours, which go against today's guidance, and where your best focus time is.

Private calendar link (iCal / ICS) optional

Where do I find this link?
  • Google Calendar: Settings → pick your calendar under “Settings for my calendars” → Integrate calendar → copy Secret address in iCal format.
  • Outlook / Microsoft 365: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → choose “Can view all details” → copy the ICS link.
  • Apple iCloud: Calendar app → share the calendar as a Public Calendar → copy the webcal:// link.

The link and your event titles stay in this browser. Chrome will ask once for permission to read your calendar provider's site. Treat the link like a password: anyone with it can see your calendar.
